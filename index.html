The verification promise

Verification you can actually trust

A stranger is coming into your home. “Trust us” isn't good enough — so we verify everything, and we show our work.

  • PSW certificate or diploma from an accredited Ontario program
  • Current CPR + First Aid certification
  • Vulnerable Sector Check — police background check within 12 months
  • HSCPOA registration — Ontario's official PSW registry
  • Government photo ID + professional references we actually call

And because PSWs earning hourly wages can't reasonably carry their own liability policies, Love-Care-Support carries a blanket policy covering every PSW during every booked visit.

What we do

Care for everyday life

Non-medical home support — the everyday help that keeps seniors safe and independent at home.

Included services

  • Personal care — bathing, dressing, grooming
  • Mobility support — transfers, walking assistance
  • Meal preparation and feeding assistance
  • Companionship and conversation
  • Light housekeeping related to care
  • Dementia and Alzheimer's support (experienced PSWs)

Not included — by law

PSWs are not nurses. To keep everyone safe and legal, our PSWs never:

  • Administer medications
  • Perform wound care or injections
  • Do nursing or clinical assessments

Need nursing care? We'll tell you honestly and point you toward the right provider.
