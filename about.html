Our story

Care is personal. So are we.

Love-Care-Support exists for one reason: nobody should have to gamble when inviting a caregiver into their home.

Why we exist

Finding trusted home care shouldn't be this hard

Every family we talk to tells some version of the same story: an aging parent who needs help, weeks of phone tag with agencies, and no real way to know who's walking through the front door.

At the same time, Ontario's Personal Support Workers — the people doing this essential, intimate work — are underpaid, undervalued, and given little control over their own schedules.

Love-Care-Support was built to fix both sides at once: families get verified, insured PSWs they can book in minutes — and PSWs keep the lion's share of what families pay, choose their own hours, and get treated like the professionals they are.

What makes us different

Three promises, kept on every booking

Not slogans — operating rules we hold ourselves to.

✓

The verification promise

No PSW takes a booking until they've cleared all five checks: accredited PSW certificate, current CPR + First Aid, a Vulnerable Sector Check within 12 months, HSCPOA registration, and government ID plus references we actually call. No exceptions, no shortcuts.

◈

Insured bookings

PSWs earning hourly wages can't reasonably buy their own liability policies — so we carry a blanket liability policy that covers every PSW during every booked visit. If something goes wrong, you're protected.

♥

PSWs treated fairly

Our PSWs are independent contractors in more than name: they set their own hours, choose the jobs they accept, and keep roughly 80% of every booking — among the fairest splits in home care. Great care starts with caregivers who feel respected.

What we stand for

Our values

The standard behind every decision we make.

🤝

Dignity first

Every client is someone's parent, grandparent, or partner. We treat them — and their homes — with the respect we'd want for our own family.

🔍

Trust, verified

Trust isn't claimed, it's demonstrated. We verify credentials, check backgrounds, and show our work — because “trust us” is never enough.

⚖️

Fairness both ways

Families deserve honest pricing with no surprises. PSWs deserve the lion's share of what families pay and real control over their work.

🏠

Home is sacred

There's no place like home — and most seniors want to stay in theirs. Our job is to make that possible, safely and beautifully.
